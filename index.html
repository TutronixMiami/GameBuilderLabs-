01 — The creator journey

Every great game begins with a small idea.

We give young creators the tools and guidance to follow that idea all the way into a world they can share.

GameBuilder Labs is hands-on by design. Students learn fundamentals through real creative decisions: how a character moves, why a level feels fun, and what makes a system work.
